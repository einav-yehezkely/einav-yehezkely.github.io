Johnny – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Johnny is a personal, non-commercial voice assistant built and used by a single person (its owner) on their own device. It is not offered to the public.

Google Calendar data

With the owner's permission, Johnny reads events and birthdays from the owner's Google Calendar and creates or deletes events only when the owner asks it to by voice. Calendar data is used only to answer the owner's own requests and to create reminders for the owner's upcoming events.

What is stored

Data is stored in the owner's private database: short conversation context (deleted after one day), reminders, voice notes the owner chose to save, and IDs of events Johnny created (so it can undo them). Voice recordings are not stored.

Sharing

Data is never sold or shared with third parties. Requests are processed by Google's Gemini API and Google Cloud Text-to-Speech to understand speech and produce spoken replies. Johnny's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
